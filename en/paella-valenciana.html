Authentic wood-fired Valencian paella

Real Valencian paella: chicken, rabbit, garrofó and bajoqueta, over orange-wood fire. Book and pre-order it.

Valencian paella takes few ingredients and a lot of craft: chicken, rabbit, garrofó beans, bajoqueta, tomato, paprika, saffron, water, salt, olive oil and Valencia D.O. rice. At Yerga we cook it over orange-tree wood, which gives a lively heat and an aroma gas never will.

It is cooked to order for a minimum of two people. Choose your paella when you book and it will be just right, with its socarrat, when you sit down.
