This is how a paella is made at Yerga, step by step.

  1. 01 · Fire

    Embers of orange-tree wood.

  2. 02 · Sofrito

    Olive oil, tomato and paprika colour the pan.

  3. 03 · Ingredients

    Chicken, rabbit, garrofó and bajoqueta.

  4. 04 · Stock and rice

    The stock rises and the rice is scattered in a cross, as tradition demands.

  5. 05 · Socarrat

    The stock evaporates and the edge turns golden.

Rice with soul

A story we cook and want to share

Yerga is my grandmother's surname. She taught me that love is shown with food, that the table can always be a little bigger, and that a hot meal can change someone's day.

That humility, that warmth, that “there's always room for one more” is the soul of Yerga.
